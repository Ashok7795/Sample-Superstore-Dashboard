# Sample-Superstore-Dashboard( Interactive Dashboard Creation Using MS Excel)
## Project Objective
The primary goal of this project is to analyze the performance of products across different dimensions, including Model, Category, Sub-Category, Region, and Symbol. The project aims to identify trends, patterns, and performance insights by analyzing data over days, months, and years.
## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/Ashok7795/Sample-Superstore-Dashboard/blob/main/Sample%20-%20Superstore.xls">Dataset</a>
## Data description
•	Orders — the core transactional dataset, containing 9,994 order line items with customer, product, sales and profit details.
•	People — maps each of the four sales regions (Central, East, South, West) to its Regional Manager.
•	Returns — a list of 800 order IDs that were returned by customers.
•	KPI / Sheet1 / Sheet2 — supporting calculation sheets used to build summary metrics for the dashboard.
•	Report — the final dashboard/report sheet containing the charts and KPI cards.
## Questions (KPIs)
- Total sales By region
- Average sales by category
- Total orders by category and shipment mode
- Top 10 products
- Total orders by shipment mode
- Show how much sales has been done by each category
- Create the chart to display profit over months

## Cleaning Process

The dataset was cleaned and prepared before visualization to ensure accuracy and consistency.

The following data preprocessing steps were performed:

- Imported the dataset into Power BI.
- Verified data types for all columns.
- Converted Order Date and Ship Date into Date format.
- Removed duplicate records, if any.
- Checked for missing or null values.
- Corrected inconsistent data formats.
- Renamed column headers where necessary for better readability.
- Created a Date hierarchy for Year, Quarter, Month, and Day analysis.

## Final Result

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to visualize the business performance of the Sample Superstore.

The dashboard includes:

- KPI Cards displaying Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Quantity Sold.
- Sales Trend Analysis over time.
- Category-wise Sales and Profit comparison.
- Sub-Category Performance Analysis.
- Region-wise Sales Distribution.
- State-wise Sales Map.
- Customer Segment Analysis.
- Monthly and Yearly Sales Trends.
- Interactive slicers for Region, Category, Segment, and Date.

## Output

The dashboard successfully presents meaningful business metrics through interactive visualizations.

Key outputs include:

- Total Sales generated by the business.
- Overall Profit earned.
- Quantity of products sold.
- Best-performing Categories and Sub-Categories.
- Region-wise sales comparison.
- Monthly and yearly sales growth.
- High-profit and low-profit products.
- Customer segment contribution to total sales.
- Sales distribution across different states.

## Insights

The analysis of the Sample Superstore dataset provides several valuable business insights:

- Technology category generated the highest sales and profit.
- Office Supplies recorded consistent sales throughout the year.
- Some Furniture products generated high sales but low profit due to heavy discounts.
- The West region contributed the highest revenue.
- Certain states showed strong sales performance while others had lower profitability.
- Consumer customers contributed the largest share of total sales.
- High discounts negatively affected overall profitability.
- Sales showed seasonal trends with higher performance during specific months.
- A small number of products contributed significantly to total revenue, indicating top-performing products.

## Conclusion

- The Sample Superstore Dashboard effectively transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights. It enables stakeholders to monitor business performance, identify profitable areas, recognize improvement opportunities, and make informed strategic decisions. The dashboard serves as a valuable business intelligence tool for analyzing retail sales performance.
